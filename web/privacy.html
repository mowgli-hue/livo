🔒 Livo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Livo is built to respect your privacy. In plain language:

What we store

Your profile, calendar, habits, goals, moods, memories and photos are saved only on your device (in your browser/app local storage). We do not upload them to a server, and we cannot see them.

Location

If you choose "Use my location," your device shares your coordinates only to centre nearby suggestions and directions. We do not store or transmit your location to us.

Live data services

To show real restaurants, places and events, Livo's server fetches data from Google Places, Ticketmaster, and (for the assistant) Anthropic. Search terms such as a city name are sent to these services to return results, and are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not send them your personal data.

No ads, no selling data

Livo shows no advertising and never sells your information.

Your control

You can export or permanently delete all your data at any time from Settings → Your data.

Children

Livo is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13.
