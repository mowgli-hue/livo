🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Livo is built to respect your privacy. In plain language:

What we store

Your profile, calendar, habits, goals, moods, memories and photos are saved only on your device (in your browser's local storage). We don't upload them to a server, and we can't see them.

Location

If you tap "Use my location," your device shares your coordinates only to centre nearby suggestions and directions. We don't store or transmit your location to us.

Live data services

To show real restaurants, places and events, Livo's server fetches data from Google Places, Ticketmaster, and (for the assistant) Anthropic. Your search terms (like a city name) are sent to these services to return results, governed by their own privacy policies. We don't send them your personal data.

No ads, no selling data

Livo shows no ads and never sells your information.

Your control

You can export or permanently delete all your data anytime from Settings → Your data.
