Frequently asked

Do I need an account?
No. Livo works fully without one — your plans, calendar, habits, goals and memories are saved on your device. Creating an account is optional and only used to back up and sync your data across your devices.

How does location work?
When you tap "Use my location," Livo sends your coordinates to our server only to find restaurants, trails and events near you and to open directions. Your location isn't stored or used for tracking.

The assistant / places aren't loading.
Livo needs an internet connection for live restaurants, events and the voice/chat assistant. If results don't appear, check your connection and try again — the app still works offline for your saved plans.

How do I use voice?
Open the assistant and tap the microphone. The first time, allow microphone and speech access. Tap the circle to talk; Livo transcribes what you say and can reply out loud.

How do I delete my data?
Go to Settings → Your data → Delete all data. This permanently removes everything stored on your device. If you have an account and want your synced backup removed too, email us.

How do I report a problem or suggest a feature?
Email us at mowgli@junglelabsworld.com — we read every message.
