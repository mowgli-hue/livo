## Full description (App Store & Play)

**Livo is the friendly companion that plans your life and makes it feel a little more lively — and a lot less lonely.**

Just tell the assistant your day in plain words — "gym at 7, work 9 to 5, lunch at 1, evening walk, cook dinner, read before bed" — and Livo builds your schedule and saves it. Open it in the morning for a full day, in the evening for just tonight.

**Plan your time off**
• Weekend plans matched to your mood, budget and who you're with
• Bigger trips with full per-person budgets (flights + hotel + activities)
• Nature stays across the country — cabins, lakeside lodges, glamping
• Group & office events by headcount, with venues that fit

**Discover what's around you**
• Real restaurants with live ratings & reviews
• Hidden gems, scenic viewpoints, waterfalls & swimming holes
• Campfire-friendly spots and beautiful road trips
• Live events, concerts and festivals with ticket links
• Everything one tap from Maps & directions in your travel mode

**Make it yours**
• A daily "spark" and a character guide to nudge you out the door
• "For You" picks that learn what you love
• Mood check-ins, habit streaks and life goals
• Capture photo memories — keep them private or share with your wanderers
• A calendar that's all yours, that syncs across your devices

**Roam together**
Share a plan and invite friends to join the same weekend — they RSVP with one tap.

Livo works beautifully on its own, and gets even better with an account that syncs everything across your devices.

Plan it. Live it. Remember it. 🌿
